Who We Are

Gaddiannaram Utsav Samithi is a community festival committee that organises the annual Vinayak Ganesh Chaturthi celebrations at Gaddiannaram, Dilsukhnagar, Hyderabad. The committee manages the pandal, daily pooja schedule, prasadam distribution and cultural programmes for the duration of the festival.

About the Laddu Prasad Pass

During the festival we prepare a limited quantity of Laddu Prasad for distribution at the pandal counter. To help us plan quantities accurately and avoid crowding, devotees can reserve their Laddu Prasad in advance by booking a Festival Laddu Booking Ticket (Laddu Prasad Pass) for ₹20 per pass.

  • Each pass entitles the holder to collect one Laddu Prasad at the Vinayak Chaturthi Pandal / Event counter.
  • The pass is issued digitally as a pass number and delivered on WhatsApp instantly after payment is confirmed.
  • The pass is valid only for the current festival duration and is non-transferable.
